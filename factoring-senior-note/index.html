Why Colombian Invoice Receivables?

We Buy Confirmed Payment Obligations - Not SME Loans

Invoice factoring is the purchase of RADIAN-registered trade receivables at a discount. The Trust holds a legally enforceable claim against the corporate payer.

How It Works

The Trust purchases registered trade receivables from Colombian SME suppliers at a discount. The SME receives immediate COP liquidity; the Trust holds the receivable and its associated rights.

Ley 1231 de 2008 governs Colombian factoring. Every eligible invoice is registered in RADIAN, Colombia's electronic invoice registry for invoices that circulate as securities.

Key Distinction

We do not lend to SMEs. We advance against confirmed payment obligations of established Colombian corporate payers, including representative names presented in the approved deck such as Cemex, Tecnoglass, Almacenes Éxito, and Bimbo.

Credit risk = the PAYER, not the SME seller.

RADIAN Platform

Operated by DIAN, RADIAN records the lifecycle and ownership events of electronic invoices that circulate as securities. It supports traceability of the legitimate holder and the invoice's availability for negotiation.

Process

Every Invoice Passes Four Stages Before a Single Dollar Is Deployed

Digital-first, multi-step underwriting. Credit is assessed on the corporate payer, not the SME seller.

01COMMERCIAL ORIGINATION
Digital-first sourcing across the Colombian SME market. CEO + 2 commercial executives. Active pipeline: COP 80B+ in approved or near-approved opportunities. Focus: manufacturing, food & beverage, services, industrials.
02INVOICE VERIFICATION
Credit scoring is distinct from origination. RADIAN registration is confirmed; corporate obligor identity and credit standing are verified; tenor, amount, and due date are validated; obligor classified Tier 1 / Tier 2 / Tier 3.
03CREDIT UNDERWRITING
Underwriting is a separate approval function. Credit assessment on the PAYER, concentration limits checked against the portfolio, Trust purchase / advance approved, blank promissory note signed at origination.
04ACTIVE MONITORING
Collections and servicing are separate operating functions. Real-time tracking of status and payment date, collections follow-up, monthly portfolio health reporting, early-warning triggers, and secondary market exit path.
